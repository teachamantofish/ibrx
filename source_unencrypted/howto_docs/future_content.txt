An institution can short shares they own without borrowing shares through a strategy known as "short selling against the box." This technique involves short selling shares that the investor already owns. Historically, this was used as a way to hedge against the downside risk of a long position in the same stock; the investor holds a long position while taking an equivalent short position in the same shares, effectively neutralizing their position's market exposure.
