Primary strategy

  • Options only
  • Buy low, sell high (BLSH)
  • BLSH requires legging into positions
  • Anchor position with 1-2 year LEAPs
  • Buy long term ITM; sell short term OTM
  • Always roll short up/out for credit
  • Sell 20-30% of position on spikes
  • Buy back in on 10-20% dips
  • Trade balanced--never naked short/long
  • Grow position by rolling profits

Risks

  • Psychological weakness such as FOMO
  • Black swan events
  • Sector and market risk
  • Shorts limit profit on huge squeeze
  • Large moves in wrong direction while legging

Known mistakes

  • FOMO: e.g., sold at 10.64 and bought 10.24 in October
  • Failure to sell on spikes
  • Buying back in too early
  • Not keeping enough cash primed
  • Too much near-term trading
