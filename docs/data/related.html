NantWorks, LLC

Relationship: Separate company and disclosed related party. ImmunityBio’s founder and executive chairman, Patrick Soon-Shiong, founded NantWorks and has a controlling interest in it.

  • Shared-services agreement: ImmunityBio recorded $2.4 million in related-party service and reimbursement expense in the first half of 2026.
  • Culver City facility license: $1.8 million expense in the first half of 2026.
  • Leases with NantWorks-controlled entities: $15.0 million related-party lease liabilities at June 30, 2026; $2.3 million lease expense in the first half of 2026.

Source: ImmunityBio Q2 2026 Form 10-Q, Note 12.

Nant Capital, LLC

Relationship: Separate Soon-Shiong-affiliated financing entity; not an ImmunityBio subsidiary.

  • Holds ImmunityBio’s December 2024 related-party convertible note, with $480 million principal outstanding at June 30, 2026 and a December 2027 maturity.
  • The note is convertible at $5.4270 per share. Nant Capital converted $25 million of principal into 4,606,596 ImmunityBio shares in March 2026.
  • In September 2026, Nant Capital led PDS Biotechnology’s PIPE financing; see PDSB below.

Sources: ImmunityBio Q2 2026 Form 10-Q; Yahoo Finance, Sept. 15, 2026.

NANT Global Finance, Inc.

Relationship: Separate Delaware financial-services holding company associated with Soon-Shiong; not listed as an ImmunityBio subsidiary in the latest 10-Q.

Its January 2026 announcement describes a blockchain-enabled capital-markets platform for digital securities and tokenized trading. It lists Soon-Shiong as an advisory-board member.

Watch item, not an announced IBRX event: the platform’s general tokenization plans could raise a future question about tokenized IBRX shares or options. No IBRX-specific proposal, authorization, or timeline was found in the cited materials.

Sources: NANT Global Finance announcement, Jan. 8, 2026; ImmunityBio Q2 2026 Form 10-Q.

PDS Biotechnology (NASDAQ: PDSB)

Relationship: Separate biotech company; no PDSB subsidiary relationship to ImmunityBio was identified.

  • Nant Capital led PDSB’s PIPE, which raised about $11.3 million at the September 14, 2026 initial closing, with up to $11 million more tied to a Phase 3 protocol milestone.
  • Soon-Shiong joined PDSB’s board. NantWorks received a one-year exclusive right to negotiate an exclusive license to PDS0101.

This is a relationship through Soon-Shiong, Nant Capital, and NantWorks, not a disclosed ImmunityBio investment or partnership.

Sources: PDSB company announcement (via Yahoo Finance); Yahoo Finance coverage.
